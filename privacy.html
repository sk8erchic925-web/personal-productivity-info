Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026.

This personal-use application accesses authorized Google spreadsheet data solely to organize and automate spreadsheet-based tasks.

Google user data is processed only as needed to provide these functions, using authorized service providers. Access credentials are protected using restricted access controls. This informational website does not request authorization or collect spreadsheet data.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or disclosed to unrelated third parties. Service providers process data as necessary to operate the application.

Spreadsheet data is retained in the owner's Google account until deleted by the owner. Operational records are subject to service-provider retention controls. The owner can revoke authorization through Google Account connections and delete stored credentials and data through the respective services.

Privacy inquiries may be directed to the support contact on the Google OAuth consent screen.
